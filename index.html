The Sound of Durgapur

Forged in the industrial heartland of Durgapur, West Bengal, CALCINATION - THE BAND® combines high-voltage guitar riffs, heavy percussion, and refined compositions to create an intense auditory signature.

Derived from the chemical process of heating minerals to extreme temperatures for pure refinement, our music reflects that same furnace-tested energy—raw, pure, and unapologetic.
